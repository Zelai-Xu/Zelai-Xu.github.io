About

I am a postdoctoral researcher at École Normale Supérieure in Paris, working at the interface of soft matter, fluid mechanics, computational mechanics, and biophysics.

My research combines mathematical modelling, numerical simulation, and continuum mechanics to investigate the mechanics and dynamics of complex soft materials and biological systems.

Research focus. My research interests span multiphase flows, rheology, poroelasticity, hydrogel mechanics, interfacial phenomena, and biophysical modelling. I am particularly interested in developing quantitative computational models that connect microscopic or mesoscale mechanisms with observable macroscopic dynamics.

Research

Soft Matter and Complex Fluids

Mechanics and dynamics of soft and structured materials under deformation and flow.

Rheology · Yielding · Thixotropy · Gelation

Hydrogel–Fluid Interactions

Coupled mechanics and transport in deformable, porous hydrogels interacting with surrounding fluids.

Poroelasticity · Swelling · Permeability · Interfacial phenomena

Computational Fluid Mechanics

Development and application of numerical methods for multiphase flows and moving interfaces.

FEM · ALE · Diffuse-interface methods · Moving contact lines

Biophysical Modelling

Mathematical and computational approaches to transport, signalling, and mechanics in biological systems.

Calcium signalling · Cellular dynamics · Mathematical modelling
